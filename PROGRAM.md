## 2. Mon / Wed / Fri — Full Recommended Routine (No Bands / No Barbells)

### Step A: Dynamic Warm-Up (5–8 mins — Zero Bands/Weights Needed)
1. **Towel or Broomstick Shoulder Dislocates (Yuri's Warmup):** `5–10 reps` (hold a bath towel or broomstick wide with straight arms, pass overhead and behind back)
2. **Squat Sky Reaches:** `5–10 reps` per side (deep squat, grab one ankle, twist and reach other arm to the ceiling)
3. **GMB Wrist Prep:** `10–20 reps` on floor (palms down, fingers pointing back, knuckles, palms up)
4. **Deadbugs:** `30 seconds` (lie on back, lower back pressed flat to floor, alternate extending opposite arm & leg)
5. **Arch Hangs (on Pull-up Bar):** `8–10 reps` (hang with straight arms, pull shoulder blades down & arch upper back)
6. **Parallel Bar Dip Support Hold:** `30–60 seconds` (straight arms on dip setup, push shoulders down away from ears, hollow core)

---

### Step B: Strength Work (3 Pairs)
> **Pairing Rule:** Do **A1** → Rest **90 seconds** → Do **A2** → Rest **90 seconds** → Repeat for **3 total sets** before moving to the next pair.
>
> **Double Progression Rule:** Work in the rep range shown for each exercise. Add 1 rep to your weakest set each session. When every set hits the top of the range with clean form, move to the next step at the bottom of the range. If any set falls **below** the bottom of the range, use the easier step (or add slow negatives) until you can do the minimum for all 3 sets.
>
> *(Leave 1–2 reps in reserve on legs on Friday to keep them fresh for Saturday's long run.)*

### Step C: Core Triplet
2–3 rounds, 60s rest between exercises. Ladders below.

*Avoid:* Hips sagging or piking up to shorten the lever; head jutting forward to meet the bar.

*Avoid:* Passive resting on lumbar spine; looking up instead of neutral neck.

## 3. Tue / Thu / Sat — Running

Distances are in the weekly overview above. Intensity rules:

* **Easy run (Tue):** conversational, `HR < 148 bpm`. Optional `4x 20s` smooth strides at the end (`40s` walk/jog between).
* **Tempo (Thu):** `2 km` easy warm-up (`< 145 bpm`) → brisk tempo (`155–165 bpm`) → `2 km` easy cool-down.
* **Long run (Sat):** first `2 km` extra easy (`< 145 bpm`), then settle into `148–155 bpm`.
* **Volume:** don't increase weekly distance by more than ~10% week to week. The app's weekly volume table flags bigger jumps.

## 5. Nutrition & Recovery Principles
*(Your personal numbers — target weight, TDEE, calorie target — live in `data.json` → `meta`.)*

* **Rate of loss:** aim for `0.5–1.0%` of body weight per week. Faster than that makes it harder to keep muscle. The dashboard shows your current rate from the weigh-in trend.
* **Weigh-ins:** daily, same conditions (morning, after the bathroom). Judge progress by the 7-day average.
* **Protein:** `1.6–2.2 g per kg` of body weight per day.
* **Sleep:** `7.5–8.0 hours / night`. If resting HR trends up several bpm for a few days, take an easier day.

## 6. Future Optimization Notes (Phase 2 — 100% Equipment-Free)
*Once adapted to the 3x/week Full RR (Weeks 4+), layer in these bodyweight-only upgrades:*

1. **5-Minute Bodyweight "V-Taper & Arm" Finisher (Post-RR on Mon/Wed/Fri):**
   * **Side & Rear Delts (No Bands/Dumbbells):** **Side Plank Powell Raises** or **Wall Lateral Deltoid Presses** or **Wide-Grip Inverted Face Pulls / Y-Raises** on your row setup (`2–3 x 12–15 reps`).
   * **Biceps & Triceps Isolation (No Weights):** **Bodyweight Tricep Extensions (Skullcrushers)** against your dip/row bar paired with **Underhand Chin-Up Bar Bicep Curls** (heels on floor/chair, curling forehead to the bar) (`2–3 x 10–15 reps`).
2. **Stretch-Mediated Hypertrophy & Lengthened Partials (*Wolf et al., 2023*):**
   * Use a **2–3 second lowering phase** and a **1-second pause at the bottom stretch** on Pull-ups, Dips, Rows, Push-ups, and Split Squats.
   * On **Set 3**, after full-range failure (`0 RIR`), do **3–5 Lengthened Partials** in the bottom stretched half of the movement.
3. **Expanded Hypertrophy Rep Ranges (`8–12 reps` on Assistance Compounds):**
   * Keep Pull-ups & Dips at `3x5–8`, and push Rows, Push-ups, and Split Squats up to `3x8–12` before leveling up leverage variations.
4. **80/20 Polarized Running Heart Rate (*Seiler, 2010*):**
   * Keep the **Tuesday easy run** strictly `< 148 bpm` (Zone 2) and the **Saturday long run** `≤ 155 bpm`, saving `155–175 bpm` threshold work for **Thursday**. The app flags runs that go over these caps.
