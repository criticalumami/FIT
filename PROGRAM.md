*(100% Equipment-Free / Bodyweight Only — Using Only Pull-Up Bar, Dip Setup & Row Setup)*

**Goal:** Cut from `80.0 kg` → `73.0–74.0 kg` while building/preserving upper & lower body muscle mass and improving 10 km running performance.
**Start Date:** Monday, Oct 19, 2026 (Full RR Day 1)

## 2. Mon / Wed / Fri — Full Recommended Routine (No Bands / No Barbells)

### Step A: Dynamic Warm-Up (5–8 mins — Zero Bands/Weights Needed)
1. **Towel or Broomstick Shoulder Dislocates (Yuri's Warmup):** `5–10 reps` (hold a bath towel or broomstick wide with straight arms, pass overhead and behind back)
2. **Squat Sky Reaches:** `5–10 reps` per side (deep squat, grab one ankle, twist and reach other arm to the ceiling)
3. **GMB Wrist Prep:** `10–20 reps` on floor (palms down, fingers pointing back, knuckles, palms up)
4. **Deadbugs:** `30 seconds` (lie on back, lower back pressed flat to floor, alternate extending opposite arm & leg)
5. **Arch Hangs (on Pull-up Bar):** `8–10 reps` (hang with straight arms, pull shoulder blades down & arch upper back)
6. **Parallel Bar Dip Support Hold:** `30–60 seconds` (straight arms on dip setup, push shoulders down away from ears, hollow core)

---

### Step B: Strength Work (3 Pairs — 3 Sets of 5–8 Reps)
> **Pairing Rule:** Do **A1** → Rest **90 seconds** → Do **A2** → Rest **90 seconds** → Repeat for **3 total sets** before moving to Pair B.
> **Double Progression Rule (`3x5` → `3x8`):** Start a bodyweight variation where you can do at least `5, 5, 5`. Add 1 rep per workout until you hit **`8, 8, 8`** with clean form, then move to the next bodyweight leverage variation!

#### Pair A: Pull-Ups + Squats (90s rest between exercises)
* **A1. Pull-Up Progression (`3 sets x 5–8 reps` — Pull-Up Bar):**
  * **Level 1:** Negative Pull-Ups (jump to top, lower over 3–5s)
  * **Level 2:** **Full Pull-Ups (or Chin-Ups)**
  * **Level 3:** **L-Sit Pull-Ups** (hold legs straight out at 90° while pulling up — adds massive core & lat load without needing weights)
  * **Level 4:** **Arch Hanger Pull-Ups** / **Typewriter Pull-Ups** / **Archer Pull-Ups**
* **A2. Squat Progression (`3 sets x 5–8 reps per leg` — Pure Bodyweight):**
  * **Level 1:** Full-Depth Bodyweight Squats (`3x8–12`)
  * **Level 2:** Split Squats (stationary lunges)
  * **Level 3:** **Bulgarian Split Squats** (rear foot elevated on a chair/couch, deep stretch at bottom)
  * **Level 4:** **Beginner Shrimp Squats** (stand on one leg, hold rear ankle behind you, lower until rear knee kisses the floor)
  * **Level 5:** **Intermediate / Advanced Shrimp Squats** (both hands behind back) or **Pistol Squats** (non-working leg held straight out in front)

#### Pair B: Dips + Hinges (90s rest between exercises)
* **B1. Dip Progression (`3 sets x 5–8 reps` — Dip Setup):**
  * **Level 1:** Negative Dips (3–5s lowering)
  * **Level 2:** **Parallel Bar Dips** (lower until shoulders are below elbows)
  * **Level 3:** **L-Sit Dips** (legs held out in an L-sit or tuck L-sit during the dip) / **Korean Dips** / **Elevated Pike Push-Ups → Wall Handstand Push-Ups**
* **B2. Hinge Progression (`3 sets x 5–8 reps` — Pure Bodyweight, No Barbell/Band):**
  * **Level 1:** Bodyweight Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift (SL-RDL — balance on one leg, hinge at hips until torso & back leg are parallel to floor)
  * **Level 2:** **Slick Floor / Towel Sliding Leg Curls** (lie on back on smooth floor with heels on a towel/socks, bridge hips up, slide heels out and curl back in using hamstrings)
  * **Level 3:** **Single-Leg Sliding Hamstring Curls** (or **Elevated Hip Thrusts — Single-Leg** with upper back on couch and foot on a chair)
  * **Level 4:** **Eccentric Nordic Hamstring Curl Negatives** (anchor ankles under couch/bed or heavy furniture, lower torso slowly over 4–5s, catch with hands and push back up) → **Full Nordic Curls**
  * *(Note: Do Level 1/2 SL-RDLs or Sliding Leg Curls on Fridays to keep hamstrings fresh for Saturday's 10 km run).*

#### Pair C: Rows + Push-Ups (90s rest between exercises)
* **C1. Row Progression (`3 sets x 5–8 reps` — Row Setup):**
  * **Level 1:** Incline Rows
  * **Level 2:** **Horizontal Inverted Rows** (body straight, heels on floor, pull chest to bar)
  * **Level 3:** **Feet-Elevated Inverted Rows** (feet elevated on a chair at bar height) or **Wide Rows** (elbows flared out 90° to hit upper back/rear delts)
  * **Level 4:** **One-Arm / Archer Rows** or **Tuck Front Lever Rows** (hang under pull-up bar with knees tucked, row body up)
* **C2. Push-Up Progression (`3 sets x 5–8 reps` — Floor):**
  * **Level 1:** **Full Push-Ups** (hands shoulder-width, hollow body, chest touches floor)
  * **Level 2:** **Diamond Push-Ups** (index fingers & thumbs touching or close together)
  * **Level 3:** **Pseudo Planche Push-Ups (PPPU)** (turn fingers sideways/back, lean shoulders far forward past hands throughout the entire set)
  * **Level 4:** **Archer Push-Ups** (one arm pushes while the other stays straight out to the side)

---

### Step C: Core Triplet (3 Rounds — 60s rest between exercises, No Equipment)
1. **C1. Anti-Extension (`8–12 reps` or `30s`):**
   * **Hanging Knee Raises → Hanging Straight-Leg Raises (Toes to Bar)** on your pull-up bar *(or Towel Sliding Ab Rollouts on knees)*
2. **C2. Anti-Rotation (`8–12 reps` or `30s/side`):**
   * **Copenhagen Side Plank** (side plank with top foot/ankle resting on a chair seat, lifting hips high — works obliques + inner thigh/adductors) OR **Side Plank** (`30s/side`)
3. **C3. Extension (`8–12 reps` or `30s`):**
   * **Reverse Hyperextensions** (lie face down on a table/bed with hips on edge, lift straight legs up) OR **Arch Body Holds (Superman Holds)** on the floor (`30s`)

## 3. Tue / Thu / Sat — Running Schedule

* **Tuesday (Run 1 — Easy Aerobic + Strides, `5–6 km`, ~35–40 min):**
  * Conversational pace (`HR < 148 bpm`). Finish with `4x 20-second` smooth strides (`40s` walk/jog between).
* **Thursday (Run 2 — Tempo / Quality, `7–8 km`, ~50–60 min):**
  * First `2 km`: Easy warm-up (`HR < 145 bpm`)
  * Middle `3–4 km`: Brisk Tempo pace (`HR ~155–165 bpm`, around `6:45–7:10 /km`)
  * Final `2 km`: Easy cool-down jog (`HR < 148 bpm`)
* **Saturday (Run 3 — Long Endurance Run, `10 km+`, ~75 min):**
  * Start the first `2 km` extra easy (`HR < 145 bpm`) to warm up legs after Friday's RR, then settle into `HR 148–155 bpm`.

---

## 4. Sunday — Skill & Mobility Day (30–35 mins)
1. **Wrist & Shoulder Prep (5 min):** GMB Wrist Prep + 10 Towel/Broomstick Shoulder Dislocates
2. **Handstand Practice (10 min timer):** Chest-to-wall handstand holds (`15–30s` holds with `60s` rest) or heel-pull balance drills
3. **Crow / Crane Pose (5 min timer):** Practice `15–30s` holds on floor
4. **L-Sit & Pike Compression (5 min):** `3 sets x 15–20s` Foot-Supported or Tuck L-Sit + `3 sets x 10s` Seated Pike Leg Lifts
5. **Skin the Cat / German Hang (`2–3 sets x 3–5 slow reps`):** On pull-up bar
6. **Runner's Mobility Cool-Down (5 min):** Couch Stretch (`60s/side`) + Calf/Achilles wall stretch (`60s/side`).

## 6. Future Optimization Notes (Phase 2 — 100% Equipment-Free)
*Once adapted to the 3x/week Full RR (Weeks 4+), layer in these bodyweight-only upgrades:*

1. **5-Minute Bodyweight "V-Taper & Arm" Finisher (Post-RR on Mon/Wed/Fri):**
   * **Side & Rear Delts (No Bands/Dumbbells):** **Side Plank Powell Raises** or **Wall Lateral Deltoid Presses** or **Wide-Grip Inverted Face Pulls / Y-Raises** on your row setup (`2–3 x 12–15 reps`).
   * **Biceps & Triceps Isolation (No Weights):** **Bodyweight Tricep Extensions (Skullcrushers)** against your dip/row bar paired with **Underhand Chin-Up Bar Bicep Curls** (heels on floor/chair, curling forehead to the bar) (`2–3 x 10–15 reps`).
2. **Stretch-Mediated Hypertrophy & Lengthened Partials (*Wolf et al., 2023*):**
   * Use a **2–3 second lowering phase** and a **1-second pause at the bottom stretch** on Pull-ups, Dips, Rows, Push-ups, and Split Squats.
   * On **Set 3**, after full-range failure (`0 RIR`), do **3–5 Lengthened Partials** in the bottom stretched half of the movement.
3. **Expanded Hypertrophy Rep Ranges (`8–12 reps` on Assistance Compounds):**
   * Keep Pull-ups & Dips at `3x5–8`, and push Rows, Push-ups, and Split Squats up to `3x8–12` before leveling up leverage variations.
4. **80/20 Polarized Running Heart Rate (*Seiler, 2010*):**
   * Keep **Tuesday (`5–6 km`)** and **Saturday (`10 km`)** strictly `< 148 bpm` (Zone 2), saving `160–175 bpm` threshold/interval work for **Thursday (`7–8 km`)**.
