Recommended Routine (RR) — Visual Form Guide

Darebee-Inspired Two-Step Anatomical Diagrams (1. Start → 2. Finish), Form Cues & All Alternate Pathways

9 Exercises (3 Pairs + 1 Triplet)
Frequency: 3x / Week Full Body
Tempo: 1-0-X-0 | Sets: 3 × 5–8 (1 RIR)

1. Pairing & Triplet Structure

Pairs (1–3): Set 1 of Ex A → Rest 90s → Set 1 of Ex B → Rest 90s → Repeat for 3 sets.
Core Triplet: Ex 1 → 60s → Ex 2 → 60s → Ex 3 → 60s × 3 rounds. (Rest up to 3m if needed).

2. Tempo (1-0-X-0) & Failure-1

1s down, 0s bottom pause, X explode up, 0s top pause. Stop 1 rep short of failure on Sets 1–2 (e.g., 7-7-7 instead of 8-6-5) to maximize clean volume.

3. Progression & Red Highlights

Start a level where you can do 5,5,5. Build to 8,8,8, then level up! Red muscle shading in each diagram shows the primary target muscles & motion vector.

PART 0: FUNDAMENTAL BODY MECHANICS (REQUIRED FOR ALL MOVES) Master Hollow Body (PPT) & Scapular Control

Hollow Body & Posterior Pelvic Tilt (PPT)

Core Lock
  • How to Lock In: Tuck your tailbone under (pull belt buckle toward chin), squeeze glutes 100%, and pull lower ribs down into abs.
  • ✓ Good: Rigid cylinder from shoulders to ankles on Push-Ups, Rows, Pull-Ups, Dips, and Rollouts.
  • ✗ Avoid: Banana-back arch (flared ribs, sagging belly).

4 Scapular Positions (Shoulder Control)

Joint Safety
  • Depress (Down): Pull shoulders away from ears (Pull-Ups, Dips, Support).
  • Retract (Pinch Back): Pinch blades together (top of Rows, Arch Hangs).
  • Protract (Push Apart): Spread blades wide (top of Push-Ups, Rollouts).
  • Elevate (Shrug Up): Push shoulders into ears (Handstands).
PART 1: DYNAMIC WARM-UP — ALL 8 EXERCISES (5–10 MINS) Do Moves 1–6 Every Session + Moves 7–8 Before Squat/Hinge Pairs

1. Yuri's Shoulder Band / Towel Warmup

5–10 Reps
  • Hold a band, towel, or broomstick with a wide overhand grip.
  • Keep elbows 100% locked, shrug slightly overhead, pass behind back to glutes, then reverse smoothly.
  • ✓ Good: Ribs tucked down. ✗ Bad: Bending elbows.

2. Squat Sky Reaches

5–10 / Side
  • Sit into a deep squat with heels flat on the floor. Grab left ankle with left hand, pinning left knee outward with elbow.
  • Rotate upper back and reach right arm straight to the sky, following fingertips with eyes.

3. GMB Wrist Prep (4 Directions)

10+ Reps
  • On all fours with elbows locked straight and fingers spread wide:
  • (1) Fingers forward, lean shoulders past fingertips; (2) Fingers facing knees, sit hips back; (3) Back of hands on floor; (4) First-knuckle palm pulses.

4. Deadbugs

30 Seconds
  • Lie on back, arms vertical, knees 90°. Crush lower back flat into the floor (posterior pelvic tilt).
  • Extend opposite arm and leg just above floor while exhaling. ✗ Stop if lower back arches off mat!

5. Arch Hangs (Add after Negative Pull-Ups)

10 Reps
  • Hang from pull-up bar with locked straight arms. Pull shoulder blades down and together, lifting sternum toward the ceiling.
  • ✓ Good: Arch upper back while keeping abs braced.

6–8. Support Hold + Warm-Up Squat/Hinge

30s + 10 Reps
  • 6. Parallel Bar Support Hold (30s): Lock elbows, push shoulders down away from ears, hollow body.
  • 7 & 8. Easier Squat & Hinge (10 reps each): Warm up knees & hamstrings before Bulgarian Split Squats / Nordics.
STRENGTH PAIR 1A: PULL-UP PROGRESSION — MAIN LADDER & ALL ALTERNATE PATHWAYS 3 Sets × 5–8 Reps | Tempo: 1-0-X-0 | Pair with Squats (90s Rest Between Exercises)
MAIN RR PULL-UP PROGRESSION (LEVELS 1 TO 5)
Primary Muscles Highlighted in Red: Latissimus Dorsi, Lower Traps, Rhomboids, Biceps

Level 1: Scapular Pulls

3 × 5–8
  • Hang from bar with overhand shoulder-width grip and locked elbows.
  • Without bending elbows, pull shoulder blades down away from ears (reverse shrug) so body rises 2–3 inches. Pause 1–2s at top, lower smoothly.

Level 2: Arch Hangs

3 × 5–8
  • Perform a Scapular Pull, then continue retracting shoulder blades to arch your upper back until your sternum faces the bar/ceiling.
  • Keep elbows locked straight and abs/glutes tight. Build to 3×8 with 2s top pauses.

Level 3: Negative Pull-Ups

3 × 5–8 (3–5s)
  • Use a chair or jump to the top position (chin over bar, chest touching bar, shoulders depressed).
  • Hold top for 1s, then lower at an even pace over 3–5 seconds to a full dead hang. Build to 3×8 controlled 5s reps.

Level 4: Full Pull-Ups (or Chin-Ups)

3 × 5–8
  • Start in a dead hang (elbows locked). Lock legs straight in a hollow body slightly in front of torso.
  • Depress scapulae and drive elbows down into your ribs until chin clears bar (or upper chest touches bar). Lower under control to full lockout.
LEVEL 5 & ALL OFFICIAL ALTERNATE PULL-UP PATHWAYS (WEIGHTED vs. BODYWEIGHT / BWF WIKI)
Choose Your Path After Mastering 3×8 Clean Pull-Ups

Path A: L-Sit Pull-Ups (Equipment-Free)

3 × 5–8
  • From a dead hang, lift straight legs to a 90° horizontal L-Sit (or start with a Tuck L-Sit).
  • Hold the L-Sit strictly horizontal throughout every pull-up. Shifts center of gravity forward for massive lat & core overload without weights.

Path B: Weighted Pull-Ups (PDF Summary Path)

3 × 5–8
  • Attach external weight via a dip belt, weight vest, or dumbbell held between ankles/thighs.
  • Start light (+2.5 kg), build from 3×5 → 3×8 with full chest-to-bar range of motion, then increase load.

Path C: Chest-to-Bar & Arch Hanger Pull-Ups

3 × 5–8
  • High Chest-to-Bar: Pull explosively so the bar touches your lower sternum below the pecs.
  • Arch Hanger Pull-Ups: Lock in an Arch Hang and pull your sternum to the bar while maintaining that 45° thoracic arch all the way up and down.

Path D: Typewriter & Archer Pull-Ups

3 × 5–8 / Side
  • Typewriter Pull-Ups: Pull chin above wide bar, slide horizontally left-to-right across the bar keeping chin above bar level, then lower.
  • Archer Pull-Ups: Pull toward one hand while keeping the opposite arm 100% locked straight on the bar (or on a towel).
STRENGTH PAIR 1B: SQUAT PROGRESSION — MAIN LADDER & ALL ALTERNATE PATHWAYS 3 Sets × 5–8 Reps / Leg | Tempo: 1-0-X-0 | Pair with Pull-Ups (90s Rest)
MAIN RR SQUAT PROGRESSION (LEVELS 1 TO 6 — SHRIMP SQUAT PATHWAY)
Primary Muscles Highlighted in Red: Quadriceps, Gluteus Maximus, Gluteus Medius, Adductors

Level 1: Assisted Squat → Level 2: Squat

3 × 5–8
  • Level 1 (Assisted): Hold a post/frame in front of you for balance as you sit into a full-depth squat.
  • Level 2 (Full Squat): Feet shoulder-width, toes out 15–30°. Track knees over toes until hip crease drops below knee height with flat back and flat heels.

Level 3: Split Squat → Bulgarian Split Squat

3 × 5–8 / Leg
  • Split Squat (Wiki Lvl 3a): Stationary lunge with both feet on floor; rear knee kisses floor.
  • Bulgarian Split Squat (PDF Lvl 3): Elevate rear foot (laces down) on a chair behind you. Lower under control until rear knee nearly touches floor; match torso lean to front shin angle.

Level 4: Beginner Shrimp Squat

3 × 5–8 / Leg
  • Stand on one leg, lift non-working foot behind you (knee bent 90°), and reach arms forward for counterbalance.
  • Squat down until your rear knee and rear toes touch the floor at the exact same time, then drive up through the front mid-foot.

Level 5: Intermediate → Level 6: Adv. Shrimp

3 × 5–8 / Leg
  • Level 5 (Intermediate Shrimp): Hold rear ankle behind you with one hand. Lower until only your rear knee kisses the floor (rear foot stays in the air).
  • Level 6 (Advanced Shrimp): Hold rear ankle behind your back with BOTH hands as rear knee kisses floor.
ALL OFFICIAL ALTERNATE SQUAT PATHWAYS (PISTOL SQUATS, DEEP STEP-UPS & BARBELL SQUATS)
Can Be Used Alongside or Instead of Shrimp Squats at Levels 4–6

Alt Path A: Pistol Squat Progression

3 × 5–8 / Leg
  • Step 1 (Box Pistol): Sit back on one leg to touch a chair while extending free leg straight ahead; lower surface height over time.
  • Step 2 (Counterbalanced / Assisted): Hold a light book/weight forward or lightly touch a doorframe at the bottom.
  • Step 3 (Full Pistol Squat): Descend until hamstring rests on calf with working heel glued flat to the floor.

Alt Path B: Deep Step-Ups & Barbell Squats

3 × 5–8 / Leg
  • Deep Step-Ups (Wiki Alternate): Place working foot on a high box/chair. Lean torso over front knee and press all the way up. Crucial Rule: Pull bottom toes UP (dorsiflexion) so the rear leg cannot push off the floor!
  • Barbell Back / Front Squat (Gym Alternate): If a barbell is available, perform 3×5–8 Barbell Squats.
STRENGTH PAIR 2A: DIP PROGRESSION — MAIN LADDER & ALL ALTERNATE PATHWAYS 3 Sets × 5–8 Reps | Tempo: 1-0-X-0 | Pair with Hinges (90s Rest Between Exercises)
MAIN RR DIP PROGRESSION (LEVELS 1 TO 4)
Primary Muscles Highlighted in Red: Pectoralis Major, Anterior Deltoids, Triceps Brachii

Level 1: Parallel Bar Support Hold

Build to 60s
  • Jump to straight-arm support on parallel bars. Lock elbows 100% and push shoulders down away from ears (scapular depression).
  • Squeeze glutes and hold a hollow body for a solid 60-second hold before progressing.

Level 2: Negative Dips

3 × 5–8 (3–5s)
  • Start in top Support Hold. Lean torso slightly forward (~15°), keep forearms vertical, and lower slowly over 3–5 seconds until shoulders drop below elbow level.
  • Step onto floor/box to return to top support for each rep.

Level 3: Parallel Bar Dips

3 × 5–8
  • Lower under control with elbows tucked close to sides and forearms vertical until your shoulder crease passes elbow height (90°+ bend).
  • Press forcefully back to locked elbows and depressed shoulders at the top. Keep legs straight (not crossed behind butt).

Level 4: L-Sit Dips OR Weighted Dips

3 × 5–8
  • Bodyweight Path (L-Sit Dips): Hold legs straight out in front (Tuck L-Sit → Full L-Sit) throughout the entire dip set.
  • Weighted Path (Weighted Dips): Add weight progressively on a dip belt (+2.5 kg increments) once 3×8 dips are clean.
ALL OFFICIAL ALTERNATE DIP PATHWAYS (RING DIPS & OVERHEAD HANDSTAND PUSH-UP PATH)
Wiki Alternates for Gymnastic Rings OR Overhead Pressing Replacement

Alt Path A: Ring Support → Ring Dips → Bulgarian

3 × 5–8
  • Prerequisite: 60s Ring Support Hold with Rings Turned Out (RTO 45°).
  • Ring Dips: Keep rings tight against ribs as you lower to 90°, press up, and turn rings out (RTO) at the top of every rep.

Alt Path B: Overhead HSPU Path (No Dip Bars)

3 × 5–8
  • Official RR replacement if you lack dip bars: Lvl 1: Floor Pike Push-Ups → Lvl 2: Box-Elevated Pike Push-Ups → Lvl 3–4: Wall Headstand Push-Ups (HeSPU).
  • ✓ Crucial Cue: Lean forward at bottom so your head lands in front of your hands forming an Equilateral Tripod Triangle!
STRENGTH PAIR 2B: HINGE PROGRESSION — MAIN LADDER & ALL ALTERNATE PATHWAYS 3 Sets × 5–8 Reps | Tempo: 1-0-X-0 | Pair with Dips (90s Rest Between Exercises)
MAIN RR HINGE PROGRESSION (LEVELS 1 TO 5 — RDL TO NORDIC CURL PATHWAY)
Primary Muscles Highlighted in Red: Hamstrings (Biceps Femoris, Semitendinosus), Glutes, Erectors

Level 1: Romanian Deadlift (Two-Leg Hip Hinge)

3 × 5–8
  • Stand hip-width apart. Unlock knees slightly (~15°) and freeze shins vertical.
  • Brace core and push your hips straight back toward the wall behind you until your flat torso reaches parallel with the floor. Squeeze glutes to return.

Level 2: Single-Legged Deadlift (SL-RDL)

3 × 5–8 / Leg
  • Balance on one leg with a soft 15° knee bend. Hinge at the hip while extending your non-working leg straight behind you like a seesaw into a Capital "T".
  • ✓ Crucial Cue: Point the toes of your raised back foot straight down at the floor to keep your hips square!

Level 3: Banded / Eccentric Nordic Negatives

3 × 5–8 (4–5s)
  • Kneel on a pad with ankles anchored firmly under a heavy couch, bed frame, or strap.
  • Squeeze glutes into PPT so knees, hips, and shoulders form one straight line. Lower slowly over 4–5 seconds toward the floor, catch gently with hands, and push back up.

Level 4: Banded Nordic → Level 5: Full Nordic Curl

3 × 5–8
  • Level 4 (Banded / Assisted Nordic): Hold a band anchored behind you (or slide hands over an elevated box) to assist the bottom reversal.
  • Level 5 (Full Nordic Curl): Lower to the floor and curl your entire bodyweight back up using pure hamstring knee flexion with zero hip bend.
ALL OFFICIAL ALTERNATE HINGE PATHWAYS (SLIDING HAMSTRING CURLS & HIP THRUSTS)
100% Equipment-Free Bridges Between Level 2 (SL-RDL) and Level 3–5 (Nordic Curls)

Alt Path A: Slick Floor / Towel Hamstring Curls

3 × 5–8
  • Lie on back on a wood/tile floor with heels on a folded towel. Bridge hips up, slide heels out until legs are nearly straight, then dig heels down and curl them back under your glutes while keeping hips bridged high.
  • Progression: Two-Leg Sliding Curl → Single-Leg Sliding Hamstring Curl.

Alt Path B: Single-Leg Hip Thrust & Harop Curl

3 × 8–12
  • Single-Leg Shoulder-Elevated Hip Thrust: Rest upper back on couch edge, plant one foot so shin is vertical at top, tuck chin, and drive hips to full glute lockout (2s pause).
  • Harop Curl: Nordic curl with a slight hip hinge on the descent to bridge the gap to strict Nordics.
STRENGTH PAIR 3A: ROW PROGRESSION — MAIN LADDER & ALL ALTERNATE PATHWAYS 3 Sets × 5–8 Reps | Tempo: 1-0-X-0 | Pair with Push-Ups (90s Rest Between Exercises)
MAIN RR ROW PROGRESSION (LEVELS 1 TO 5)
Primary Muscles Highlighted in Red: Mid-Traps, Rhomboids, Rear Delts, Lats, Biceps

Level 1: Vertical Row → Level 2: Incline Row

3 × 5–8
  • Level 1 (Vertical): Stand facing a post/doorframe with feet close to base; lean back and pull chest to hands.
  • Level 2 (Incline Row): Use a chest-high bar at ~45° body angle. Keep body rigid and pull chest to bar.

Level 3: Horizontal Rows (Inverted Row)

3 × 5–8
  • Hang under a waist-high bar with heels on the floor and body in a straight reverse plank (glutes squeezed).
  • Depress shoulders, retract shoulder blades, and drive elbows back (~45°) until your lower chest physically touches the bar.

Level 4: Wide Rows & Feet-Elevated Rows

3 × 5–8
  • Wide Rows (PDF Lvl 4): Grip 1.5× shoulder width and flare elbows out at 90°, pulling upper chest to bar (hits rear delts & upper back).
  • Feet-Elevated Rows: Prop heels on a chair at bar height so body is horizontal at the top.

Level 5A: Weighted Inverted Rows (PDF Path)

3 × 5–8
  • Wear a weight vest or rest a weight plate on your chest (never on your belly where it causes hip sag).
  • Pull chest all the way to the bar on every rep with full scapular retraction.
ALL OFFICIAL ALTERNATE ROW PATHWAYS (ARCHER / ONE-ARM ROWS & FRONT LEVER ROWS)
100% Bodyweight Mastery Pathways After Level 4 Horizontal / Wide Rows

Alt Path A: Archer Rows → One-Arm Rows

3 × 5–8 / Side
  • Archer Rows: Row up with one arm while keeping the assisting arm 100% locked straight out to the side.
  • One-Arm Rows: Hold bar with one hand, take a wider foot stance, and row chest to bar while keeping shoulders and hips completely square.

Alt Path B: Tuck Front Lever Pulls & Rows

3 × 5–8
  • Done on Pull-Up Bar! Hang from bar, tuck knees to chest, and press down with straight arms until your back is horizontal to the floor in mid-air.
  • From that horizontal hang, row your hips/chest up toward the bar while keeping your torso parallel to the floor!
STRENGTH PAIR 3B: PUSH-UP PROGRESSION — MAIN LADDER & ALL ALTERNATE PATHWAYS 3 Sets × 5–8 Reps | Tempo: 1-0-X-0 | Pair with Rows (90s Rest Between Exercises)
MAIN RR PUSH-UP PROGRESSION (LEVELS 1 TO 5)
Primary Muscles Highlighted in Red: Pectoralis Major, Anterior Delts, Triceps, Serratus Anterior

Level 1: Vertical → Level 2: Incline Push-Up

3 × 5–8
  • Place hands on an elevated surface (wall → counter → table → chair) rather than doing knee push-ups so you train full-body hollow tension.
  • Lower until sternum touches the edge with elbows tucked ~45°, then press to full protraction.

Level 3: Full Push-Up

3 × 5–8
  • Hands shoulder-width, externally rotate arms ("screw hands into floor"). Lock pelvis in Posterior Pelvic Tilt (PPT).
  • Lower with elbows at a 45° arrow angle until chest touches floor. Press up and protract scapulae at top.

Level 4: Diamond Push-Up

3 × 5–8
  • Place hands close together directly underneath your sternum so index fingers and thumbs form a diamond (or sit 1–2" apart).
  • Keep elbows brushing your ribs as your chest lowers onto your hands, then press to full lockout and protraction.

Level 5: Pseudo Planche Push-Up (PPPU)

3 × 5–8
  • Turn fingers 45–90° outward (or backward). Lean shoulders far forward past your wrists so hands sit down near your waist/hips.
  • ✓ Crucial Rule: Maintain that forward shoulder lean at both bottom AND top without rocking backward!
ALL OFFICIAL ALTERNATE PUSH-UP PATHWAYS (ARCHER / ONE-ARM PUSH-UPS & RING PUSH-UPS)
Wiki Alternates for Unilateral Pushing Strength OR Gymnastic Rings

Alt Path A: Archer → One-Arm Push-Up

3 × 5–8 / Side
  • Archer Push-Ups: Hands 2× shoulder width. Shift weight over one hand and lower chest to that hand while keeping the assisting arm 100% locked straight.
  • One-Arm Push-Up: Straddle feet wide, place one hand under sternum, keep hips/shoulders square, and press with one arm.

Alt Path B: Ring Push-Ups → RTO Push-Ups

3 × 5–8
  • Ring Push-Ups: Lower into a deep chest stretch on floor-level rings, press up, and Turn Rings Out (RTO 45–90°) at the top.
  • RTO Push-Ups / Ring PPPU: Keep rings turned out (palms forward) throughout the entire descent AND ascent.
PART 3: CORE TRIPLET — ALL 3 CATEGORIES & ALL ALTERNATE PATHWAYS Circuit: Do C1 → 60s Rest → C2 → 60s Rest → C3 → 60s Rest × 3 Rounds (Target: 3 × 8–12 or 30s)
CORE 1: ANTI-EXTENSION PROGRESSION (RING/TOWEL AB ROLLOUTS OR HANGING LEG RAISES)
Resists Lumbar Hyperextension & Strengthens Rectus Abdominis, Transverse Abdominis & Lats

Path A: Plank → Ring / Towel Ab Rollouts

3 × 8–12
  • Lvl 1: RKC Plank (30s): Forearm plank with glutes/quads squeezed 100% and tailbone tucked (PPT).
  • Lvl 2–4: Kneeling Ab Rollouts (Rings, Wheel, or Towel on Slick Floor): Lock pelvis in PPT, slide hands forward only as far as lower back stays flat/hollow, then pull back through lats & abs.

Path B: Hanging Knee / Leg Raises (Toes-to-Bar)

3 × 8–12
  • Progression: Lying Tuck → Hanging Knee Raises → Hanging Straight-Leg Raises (90°) → Toes-to-Bar.
  • ✓ Crucial Cue: At the top of every rep, curl your tailbone/pelvis upward so your lower back rounds and your abs contract fully. Zero swinging!
CORE 2: ANTI-ROTATION PROGRESSION (BANDED PALLOF PRESS OR COPENHAGEN / SIDE PLANK)
Resists Torso Twist & Lateral Pelvic Drop — Obliques, Quadratus Lumborum, Glute Medius, Adductors

Path A: Banded Pallof Press (or Ring Pallof)

3 × 8–12 / Side
  • Stand sideways to a chest-high band anchor, pull band to sternum, press straight out in front of chest, pause 2s resisting rotation, and return.
  • Can also be performed as a single-arm Ring Pallof plank rollout.

Path B: Side Plank → Copenhagen Side Plank

3 × 8–12 or 30s
  • 100% Equipment-Free Path: Start with Side Plank (30s/side) with elbow directly under shoulder.
  • Copenhagen Plank (Knee → Ankle Lever): Rest top knee or top ankle on a chair seat, drive hips up into a straight line, and lift bottom leg off the floor for 30s holds or 8–12 hip lifts.
CORE 3: EXTENSION PROGRESSION (REVERSE HYPEREXTENSION OR ARCH BODY HOLDS / ROCKS)
Strengthens Posterior Chain — Spinal Erectors, Multifidus, Gluteus Maximus, Upper Back

Path A: Reverse Hyperextension (PDF Path)

3 × 8–12
  • Lie prone on a sturdy table, counter, or high bed with your hip crease right on the edge so legs hang down. Grip sides firmly.
  • Squeeze glutes + lower back to lift straight legs until parallel with torso (180°). Pause 1s at top, lower slowly.

Path B: Arch Body Hold → Arch Body Rocks

3 × 30s / 8–12
  • Floor Alternate: Lie face down on floor. Squeeze glutes, lock knees, and lift chest, arms, and thighs off floor so only belly rests on mat.
  • Progression: Arms behind back → Arms overhead (Superman) → Arch Body Rocks.
PART 4: OFFICIAL RR SKILL DAY ROUTINE & PHASE 2 BODYWEIGHT ISOLATION MOVES Practiced on Rest Days (e.g., Sunday) OR Before Strength Work
OFFICIAL /R/BODYWEIGHTFITNESS SKILL DAY MOVES (BALANCE, COMPRESSION & SHOULDER MOBILITY)
Focus on Motor Control & Balance — Stop Before Fatigue!

1. Wall Plank → Chest-to-Wall Handstand

5–10 Min Practice
  • Walk feet up the wall facing the wall until hands are 2–4" from baseboard (Chest-to-Wall, never back-to-wall).
  • 3 Key Cues: (1) Push floor away to elevate shoulders into ears; (2) Lock pelvis in PPT; (3) Press fingertips into floor ("heel pulls") to float toes off wall.

2. Frog Stand / Crow Pose → Crane Pose

5 Min Practice
  • Place palms shoulder-width on floor, rest knees on backs of triceps, and lean shoulders forward until feet float up. Look slightly ahead on floor.
  • Crane Pose Upgrade: Perform with 100% locked straight elbows and strong scapular protraction.

3. L-Sit Progression & Pike Compression

60s Total Hold
  • Floor/Bar L-Sit: Sit tall, place palms beside hips, lock elbows, and push shoulders DOWN hard to lift hips & legs.
  • Levels: Foot-Supported → 1-Leg Supported → Tuck L-Sit → Full L-Sit (+ 3×10s Seated Pike Leg Lifts).

4. Skin the Cat & German Hang (Pull-Up Bar)

2–3 × 3–5 Reps
  • Hang with an overhand grip and locked elbows. Tuck knees to chest and invert backward through your arms.
  • Lower slowly into a mild shoulder-extension stretch (German Hang), then pull hips up and rotate back to dead hang.
BONUS (FROM PROGRAM.MD): 5-MINUTE BODYWEIGHT V-TAPER & ARM ISOLATION FINISHER
Optional Post-RR Finisher (2–3 Sets × 10–15 Reps — Zero Dumbbells or Bands Required)

Bodyweight Tricep Extensions & Bar Bicep Curls

2–3 × 10–15
  • BW Tricep Extensions (Skullcrushers): Incline plank on bar; freeze upper arms and bend ONLY at elbows to lower forehead under bar, then extend.
  • Underhand Bar Bicep Curls: Hang under row bar with supinated grip; keep elbows high in front of torso and curl forehead to bar.

Inverted Face Pulls & Side-Plank Powell Raises

2–3 × 12–15
  • Wide-Grip Inverted Face Pulls: Lean back on row bar at 45°, flare elbows high (90°), and externally rotate shoulders to pull bar to your forehead (1s pause).
  • Side-Plank Powell Raise / Wall Lateral Press: Isolate lateral & rear delts with zero equipment.
